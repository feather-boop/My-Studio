My Studio: install on your Android tablet

1. Put this whole folder on any HTTPS web host (GitHub Pages, Netlify Drop, Cloudflare Pages).
2. Open the address in Chrome on your tablet, tap the menu (three dots), then "Install app".
   My Studio appears on your home screen with its icon, opens full screen and works offline.
3. To bring your projects over: in the current web version open Backup, save the backup file,
   then in the installed app open Backup, choose the file and tap Restore backup twice.

For a real APK or a Play Store listing: give your hosted address to pwabuilder.com and choose Android.
